<p class="essay__poem">
Vincent van Gogh said<br>
“What’s done with love is done well.”<br>
Do you live with love?<br>
-<br>
Sunlight trickling in<br>
A slept-in Sunday morning<br>
Refreshed and grateful<br>
-<br>
Finals in winter<br>
Seasonal depression hits<br>
But you fight with grit<br>
-<br>
<em>clink clink! </em>“Cheers to us!”<br>
The table blooms with laughter<br>
as friendships deepen<br>
-<br>
Not giving up on<br>
Dreams that everyone told you<br>
Never to pursue<br>
-<br>
You might not feel it<br>
But you life with so much love<br>
Every single day<br>
-<br>
Experiencing<br>
Nature, laughter, sadness, warmth<br>
of the whole wide world<br>
-<br>
Living with love<br>
Is what helps us fall in love<br>
With being alive.
</p>
